### About

I research large language models, with a background in Computer Science and Engineering from Metropolitan University, Sylhet, Bangladesh. My work asks why these models state false things in the same confident tone they use for the truth. I trace those errors to specific parts of the architecture, then test each claim with controlled, pre-registered experiments and publish the data and code.

- **Focus:** hallucination, uncertainty and the evaluation of language models
- **Methods:** architecture ablations, matched model pairs, blind double-scoring, mixed-effects models
- **Open to:** research collaboration on trustworthy and reliable AI
